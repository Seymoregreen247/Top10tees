Size Chart

Gildan G500 Heavy Cotton Tee — our standard blank

All Top 10 Tees are printed on Gildan G500 tees. Measurements below are the garment's own dimensions, laid flat — not your body measurement. For the most accurate fit, lay a tee you already own flat and compare it to the chart.

SizeSMLXL2XL3XL4XL5XL

Body Length28"29"30"31"32"33"34"35"

How to measure

  • Chest width: lay the shirt flat, measure straight across about 1" below the armhole. This number is half the full chest circumference — double it for the full measurement around the body.
  • Body length: lay the shirt flat, face down, and measure from the center back collar straight down to the bottom hem.
Fit runs classic and true to size, not slim. If you're between two sizes or prefer a roomier fit, size up.
