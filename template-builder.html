Getting these into Top 10 Tees

  1. Export your 4 colors above (one click, zipped, 4500×4500px — print/upload ready).
  2. Unzip, then in the app open MANAGE → PHOTO GALLERY → Add Photos to Gallery and upload all 4.
  3. Tag them by city (Boston / Detroit) and season right there in the gallery so they stay organized.
  4. Go to MANAGE → TOP 10 DESIGNS, find the slot, and pick your new photo from the "FROM GALLERY" dropdown — done.
